📖 Bible · Week 2
Adam & Eve
The First Family
Life in the garden · God's care · What happened when they disobeyed
🌿
⭐ Memory Verse
"But God shows his love for us in that while we were still sinners, Christ died for us."
Romans 5:8
💡
What does this mean? Even when we do wrong things (like Adam and Eve did), God still loves us. That love is so big that He sent Jesus for us. That is AMAZING love!
🌱 The Story — What Happened?
Read this carefully
1
God made the Garden of Eden 🏡
After God made the whole world in 6 days, He planted a beautiful garden called Eden. It had trees with delicious fruit, rivers, animals, and perfect weather. It was paradise — a perfect, safe, happy home.
2
God made Adam 🧍
God made the first man from the dust of the ground and breathed life into him. His name was Adam. God put Adam in charge of the garden — to look after it and name all the animals.
3
God made Eve 🧍‍♀️
God saw that Adam was lonely. So He made Eve from Adam's rib — the first woman. She was made to be his helper and companion. They were the first family! God was happy with what He made.
4
One rule 🚫
God gave Adam and Eve one rule: "You may eat from any tree in the garden — except the tree of the knowledge of good and evil. Do NOT eat from that one." It was one simple rule, given because God loved them and knew what was best.
5
The serpent's trick 🐍
A serpent (snake) said to Eve: "Did God really say you cannot eat from any tree?" The serpent told a lie — that the fruit would make them like God and that God was hiding something good. Eve listened. She ate the fruit. Then she gave some to Adam, and he ate too.
6
The consequence 😔
Adam and Eve felt ashamed and afraid for the first time. God knew what they had done. He was sad, but He was also fair. Because they broke His one rule, they had to leave the garden. Life became harder — but God still loved them and made them clothes to wear.
📚 Key Words
🌳 Eden
The beautiful garden God made. It was a perfect home.
🧍 Adam
The first man God made, from dust.
🧍‍♀️ Eve
The first woman, made from Adam's rib. The first family!
🚫 Disobey
To do something you were told NOT to do.
😔 Consequence
What happens BECAUSE of what you did.
❤️ Compassion
To care about someone even when they made a mistake.
🤔 Think About It — Big Questions
❓
Why did Adam and Eve disobey? The serpent told them a lie. He made the wrong choice sound good. Sometimes things that look good or feel exciting are not right. We must think before we act.
💜
Did God stop loving them? NO! Even after they disobeyed, God made clothes for them. This shows He still cared. Romans 5:8 tells us — God's love does NOT stop when we make mistakes.
🙏
What can WE learn? Rules exist to protect us, just like God's one rule was there to protect Adam and Eve. When we break rules, there are consequences — but we can always come back to God.

📌 What is a Sentence?
💡
A sentence is a group of words that makes complete sense on its own. Every sentence must have:
👤 Who (person/thing) 🏃 What they do (verb/action) 🔤 Capital letter at the start 🔴 Punctuation at the end
❌
NOT a sentence: "the big dog" — no action! "Ran fast." — who?
✅ IS a sentence: "The big dog ran fast." — who + action + punctuation ✓
🗂️ The 4 Types of Sentence
Click each one to learn more
🔴 Statement (tap for example)
It tells us a fact or information.
📌 Ends with a full stop (.)
✏️ Example: "The cat sat on the mat."
✏️ Example: "Adam and Eve lived in the garden."
💬 Use it to: tell someone a fact or describe something.
🔵 Question (tap for example)
It asks for information.
📌 Ends with a question mark (?)
✏️ Example: "Where did you go?"
✏️ Example: "What is your name?"
💬 Often starts with: Who, What, Where, When, Why, How.
🟡 Exclamation (tap for example)
It shows strong feeling or surprise!
📌 Ends with an exclamation mark (!)
✏️ Example: "What a beautiful garden!"
✏️ Example: "How amazing that is!"
⚠️ Important: True exclamation sentences start with What or How.
🟢 Command (tap for example)
It tells someone to do something.
📌 Ends with a full stop (.) or !
✏️ Example: "Open the door."
✏️ Example: "Sit down now!"
💬 Commands start with a VERB (action word). The subject (you) is invisible!
🔤 Punctuation Rules
.
Full Stop
Use at the end of a statement or command. "The dog barked."
?
Question Mark
Use at the end of a question. "Where is she?"
!
Exclamation Mark
Use at the end of an exclamation or strong command. "Stop!"
A
Capital Letter
ALWAYS starts every sentence. Also used for names.
⭐
Golden Rule: Every sentence starts with a CAPITAL letter and ends with a punctuation mark (. ? !).

🚚 What is 'Carrying'?
💡
Carrying (or 'regrouping') happens when a column adds up to 10 or more.
10 ones = 1 ten. So we CARRY the 1 ten to the next column.
10 tens = 1 hundred. So we CARRY the 1 hundred to the next column.
8 + 7 = 15
Write 5 in this column
Carry the 1 to the left ➡️
6 + 3 = 9
Write 9 in this column

Plants —
Parts & Functions
Root · Stem · Leaf · Flower · Seed · What each part does
🌸
🌍 Before We Start — Easy English
📖
Parts = pieces of something. A plant has PARTS — like a body has parts (arms, legs, head).
Function = what something DOES. The function of your eyes is to see. The function of a root is to drink water.
Nutrients = good things in soil that help plants grow (like vitamins for us).

☀️ What Does a Plant Need to Grow?
🌍
Plants cannot eat food like we do. They MAKE their own food! But they need 4 things to do this.
☀️
Sunlight
For making food (photosynthesis)
💧
Water
Soaked up through the roots
🌍
Nutrients
Good things in soil (like vitamins)
💨
Air (CO₂)
Breathed in through tiny holes in leaves
🔬
Big word: Photosynthesis (say it: fo-to-SIN-thuh-sis)
This is the name for how plants MAKE their own food. Leaves trap sunlight + take in water + take in air = MAKE FOOD. This food is sugar that the plant uses to grow.

🗺️ What is a Map?
💡
A map is a picture of a place from above — like a bird flying high and looking down. Maps help us find our way, understand where things are, and plan journeys.
📍 Maps show:
✅ Roads and streets
✅ Buildings (school, hospital)
✅ Parks and green spaces
✅ Rivers and water
✅ Where North/South/East/West is
❌ Maps don't show:
❌ The height of buildings
❌ What colour things are
❌ How many people are inside
❌ Moving things (cars, people)
🧭 The Compass — N, S, E, W
📌
A compass tells us which direction we are facing. Every map has a compass rose that shows which way is North. Once you know North, you can work out all the other directions!
N
S
E
W
N
North ⬆️
At the TOP of the map. The sun rises in the East and sets in the West — North is "up".
S
South ⬇️
At the BOTTOM of the map. Opposite of North.
E
East ➡️
On the RIGHT of the map. The sun rises in the East every morning.
W
West ⬅️
On the LEFT of the map. The sun sets in the West every evening.
