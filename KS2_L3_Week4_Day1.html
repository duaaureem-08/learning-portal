🌍 Words in Your Language
Multiply = ضرب (zarb) — Urdu · ضرب (darb) — Arabic. It means: add the same number again and again!
3 × 4 means "3 groups of 4" → 4 + 4 + 4 = 12. Multiplication is fast adding!
Product = حاصل ضرب (hasil zarb) — the ANSWER to a multiplication.
