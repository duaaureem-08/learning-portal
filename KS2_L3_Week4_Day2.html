Part A — Study the Facts · 8 marks
🌍 Read Each Continent. Answer the Questions.
🌏 Asia
Largest continent. Over 4 billion people — more than half of all humans live here! Pakistan, China, India are in Asia. Has the highest mountain: Mount Everest.
🌍 Africa
Second largest continent. Has the Sahara Desert — the biggest hot desert in the world. Has the River Nile — the longest river. 54 countries.
🌎 North America
Third largest continent. Includes USA, Canada, Mexico. Has the Great Lakes — largest group of freshwater lakes. Very varied climate: hot deserts to frozen north.
🌎 South America
Has the Amazon Rainforest — largest rainforest in the world. Has the Amazon River. Brazil is the largest country. Angel Falls (Venezuela) is the world's highest waterfall.
🌍 Europe
Smallest continent (not counting Antarctica or Australia). Has many countries in a small area — 44 countries! UK, France, Germany, Italy are in Europe.
🌏 Australia (Oceania)
Smallest continent (island continent). Unique animals: kangaroos, koalas, platypus. Has the Great Barrier Reef — largest coral reef in the world.
❄️ Antarctica
Coldest, driest, windiest continent. No permanent human population. Covered in ice. South Pole is here. Penguins live here!
a) Which continent has the most people? Where does your family come from?
b) Which TWO continents have the world's biggest desert AND the world's biggest rainforest? How are these two environments different?
c) If you could visit any continent, which would you choose? Give TWO reasons using facts from the cards above.
