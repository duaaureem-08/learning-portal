📖 Part B — Passage Analysis 10 marks

Read this passage from Ruth 1:16–17. Then answer ALL four questions below:

"But Ruth replied, 'Don't urge me to leave you or to turn back from you. Where you go I will go, and where you stay I will stay. Your people will be my people and your God my God. Where you die I will die, and there I will be buried. May the Lord deal with me, be it ever so severely, if even death separates you and me.'"

— Ruth 1:16–17
QuestionYour answer — use evidence from the passage
a) What does "Don't urge me to leave you" tell us about what Naomi had been asking Ruth to do? Why do you think Naomi kept asking?
b) Ruth says "Where you die I will die, and there I will be buried." What does this show about the depth of her commitment? Why is this more than just a kind gesture?
c) "Your people will be my people and your God my God." Ruth was a Moabite, not an Israelite. What makes this line so remarkable? What was she giving up?
d) "May the Lord deal with me, be it ever so severely, if even death separates you and me." Ruth uses a strong oath here. What does this tell us about her character — is she making a casual promise or a deep commitment?
💭 Part C — Critical Thinking 10 marks
3
Boaz was described as a man of "standing" and "worth" (Ruth 2:1). Yet he treated Ruth — a poor foreign widow — with great kindness. What does this tell us about Boaz's character? How does his behaviour contrast with how foreigners were often treated in the ancient world? Write 4–5 sentences.
4
Compare Orpah and Ruth. Both were in the same situation. Orpah chose to return home; Ruth chose to stay. Neither choice was wrong — but they had very different outcomes. What does this comparison teach us about the choices we make? Write 3–4 sentences using the words "loyalty," "consequence" and "faith."
5
EXTENDED: Ruth became the great-grandmother of King David, and is listed in the family line of Jesus (Matthew 1:5). Why do you think God chose to include a Moabite woman — a foreigner and a widow — in this most important family line? What does this tell us about God's character and values? Write at least 5 sentences using evidence from the story.

Read this how-to guide carefully. Then answer ALL four questions:

How to plant a seed 🌱

First, carefully choose a small pot and fill it gently with moist soil — do not pack it too tightly.
Next, slowly push your finger about one centimetre into the soil to make a small hole.
Then, gently drop one seed into the hole and softly cover it with a little soil.
After that, quietly place the pot on a sunny windowsill where it will receive plenty of light.
Finally, carefully water the soil — just a little! — and wait patiently for your seedling to appear.
