✏️ Part B — Reading Analysis & Application 10 marks
🦁 Re-read this non-fiction passage:

Why Lions Are Called the King of the Jungle
What Makes a Lion Special?
Lions are large, powerful cats that live mainly in Africa. The male lion has a thick mane of fur around his head, which makes him look bigger and more powerful. Lions are the only big cats that live in groups, called prides. A pride usually has one or two male lions, several females and their cubs.

How Do Lions Hunt?
Female lions, called lionesses, do most of the hunting. They work as a team to catch animals like zebras and wildebeest. Lions are most active at night, when their excellent eyesight helps them spot prey in the dark. A lion can run at 80 km per hour for short bursts — fast enough to catch most animals.

1a. Find ONE fact and ONE opinion from the passage. Write each one out and explain HOW you know which is which.

1d. The author writes: "A lion can run at 80 km per hour for short bursts — fast enough to catch most animals." What technique does the author use after the dash? What effect does it have on the reader?
