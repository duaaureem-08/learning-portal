✔️ Part D — Mixed True or False 5 marks
⭐ Week 11 English — Key Rules
1️⃣ First
Introduces the very first step. "First, boil the water."
2️⃣ Next / Then
Shows the following step. "Next, add the pasta."
🔄 After that
The step after. "After that, stir well."
⏰ Meanwhile
Two things at same time. "Meanwhile, heat the sauce."
🏁 Finally
The very last step. "Finally, serve and enjoy!"
🏃 -ly Adverbs
Add HOW: carefully, gently, quickly, quietly, bravely…

✔️ Part D — Mixed True or False 5 marks
⭐ Week 11 Science — Key Ideas
🧲 Magnetic
Iron, steel, nickel, cobalt are magnetic. NOT aluminium, copper, gold, wood, plastic.
🔴🔵 Poles
Every magnet has N and S. Force is strongest at the poles.
✅ Opposite = Attract
N + S pull together. Opposite poles attract.
❌ Like = Repel
N + N or S + S push apart. Like poles repel.

⭐ Week 11 Humanities — Key Ideas
🌴 Tropical
Hot all year, near equator. Very wet. Bananas, rice, cocoa. Brazil, Nigeria, Indonesia.
🌿 Temperate
Four seasons, mild. Wheat, potatoes, apples. UK, France, Pakistan.
❄️ Polar
Extremely cold, near poles. Little grows. Antarctica, Arctic, Norway.
🌡️ Climate vs Weather
Climate = long-term patterns. Weather = what's happening today.
