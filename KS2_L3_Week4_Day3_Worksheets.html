Part B — Passage Analysis & Evidence 10 marks
📖 Read the Passage. Answer ALL Questions with Evidence.
"By faith Abraham obeyed when he was called to go out to a place that he was to receive as an inheritance. And he went out, not knowing where he was going. By faith he went to live in the land of promise, as in a foreign land, living in tents with Isaac and Jacob, heirs with him of the same promise. For he was looking forward to the city that has foundations, whose designer and builder is God."

— Hebrews 11:8–10
Inheritance = ورثہ — something you receive as a gift, often from your family or from God.
Foreign land = غیر ملک — a land that is NOT your home country. It feels unfamiliar and different.
Foundations = بنیادیں — the strong base at the bottom of a building. Here it means: a city that will last forever.
a) The passage says Abraham "went out, not knowing where he was going." In your own words, explain what this tells us about the KIND of faith Abraham had. Use the verse as evidence in your answer.
💡 Think: would YOU leave home without knowing where you were going? What does this tell us about how much Abraham trusted God?
b) Abraham "lived in tents" in the land of promise — not in a permanent home. Why do you think God asked Abraham to live this way? What might God have been teaching him about trust?
c) The passage says Abraham was "looking forward to the city... whose designer and builder is God." This city is NOT on Earth. What does this tell us about what Abraham REALLY hoped for? How does this connect to Hebrews 11:1?
d) How is Abraham's faith different from how most people think? Most people say "I will believe it when I see it." What does Abraham's story teach us about how to live differently?

Part B — Analyse the Writer's Craft 10 marks
📖 Read the Passage. Answer ALL Questions.
"The tiny, trembling child stood alone at the edge of the crumbling cliff. Far below, grey waves crashed against jagged black rocks. A cold, relentless wind howled and tore at her ragged coat. She closed her eyes, breathed in the salt air, and took one careful step forward."
a) Find THREE noun phrases (noun + adjective(s)) in the passage. For each one, explain what effect the adjective creates on the reader.
💡 Example: "the crumbling cliff" — "crumbling" makes the reader feel danger and instability, as if the cliff could break at any moment.
b) The writer uses the verb "howled" for the wind. Why is "howled" a stronger choice than "blew"? What does it make you imagine?
c) "She closed her eyes, breathed in the salt air, and took one careful step forward." The adjective "careful" is the ONLY adjective in this final sentence. Why do you think the writer used only ONE adjective here instead of many? What effect does this create?
d) Rewrite the final sentence from the perspective of the child feeling BRAVE and HOPEFUL instead of scared. Change the adjectives and verbs to create a completely different mood. Then explain what you changed and why.
