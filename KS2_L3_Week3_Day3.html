✍️ Part E — Connect the Stories (8 marks)
💬 Across Weeks 1, 2 and 3 we studied: Creation, Adam & Eve, and Noah. What ONE big idea connects all three stories? Write at least FOUR sentences explaining the connection. Use the words: love, obedience, consequence, trust.
One big idea connecting all three stories is GOD'S LOVE. In Creation, God made a perfect world out of love. In Adam & Eve, even after disobedience brought consequences, God still made clothes for them — showing compassion. In Noah, God saved a righteous man because He loved and valued him. Trust and obedience are also threaded through — those who trust God (Noah) are protected; those who disobey (Adam & Eve) face consequences. But in every story, God's love does not end — it adapts, warns, rescues and renews.

🧠 Part D — Reasoning Challenge (5 marks)
Without calculating the exact answer, explain how you know that 487 + 364 must give an answer greater than 800. Use the words "hundreds", "tens" and "ones" in your explanation.
I know this because if I just look at the hundreds digits: 4 hundreds + 3 hundreds = 7 hundreds = 700. Then the tens digits: 8 tens + 6 tens = 14 tens = 140. 700 + 140 = 840, which is already over 800 before we even add the ones. So the total must be greater than 800.
A student says: "I always start addition and subtraction from the HUNDREDS column because it is the most important." Are they right? Explain clearly why the ONES column must come first.
The student is wrong. We must start from the ONES column because we need to know whether there is a carry (regrouping) to add to the tens column. If we start from the hundreds, we will not know if the ones or tens have created an extra 1 to carry over, and our answer will be wrong. The ones column affects the tens, which affects the hundreds — so we must work from right to left.

✍️ Part D — Extended Science Thinking (5 marks)
A student says: "Plants and animals are completely different — they have nothing in common." Using what you know about photosynthesis and what plants produce, explain how plants and animals are actually CONNECTED. Write at least THREE sentences.
Plants and animals are deeply connected. During photosynthesis, plants produce OXYGEN as a by-product — this is the gas all animals (including humans) breathe to survive. Plants also form the base of every food chain, providing energy to herbivores, which in turn feed carnivores. Without photosynthesis, there would be no oxygen to breathe and no food at the base of the food chain — all animal life would end. Far from being separate, plants and animals depend on each other completely.

✍️ Part D — Thinking Like a Geographer (6 marks)
Compare the UK and your home country (Pakistan). Name TWO things they have in common (e.g. both have mountains or rivers) and TWO things that are DIFFERENT. Use geography vocabulary: mountain, river, coastline, capital city, border, region.
Similar: Both the UK and Pakistan have mountain ranges (the Himalayas and Karakoram in Pakistan; the Scottish Highlands and Snowdonia in the UK). Both also have major rivers — the Indus in Pakistan, the Thames and Severn in the UK. Different: The UK is an island with a long coastline on all sides; Pakistan has only a small coastline along the Arabian Sea to the south. Also, the UK has four separate countries within it with different capitals; Pakistan is one country with Islamabad as its single capital city.
