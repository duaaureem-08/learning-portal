1. Read this passage carefully. Then answer ALL questions:

"The market was loud and busy, but Sara loved it. She ran between the stalls because she wanted to find the best fruit. When she finally found the mango seller, she smiled so brightly that the old man gave her an extra one for free."
QuestionYour answer — quote the text
a) Find ALL four conjunctions in the passage. Write them and explain what job each one is doing in that sentence.
b) "The market was loud and busy, but Sara loved it." Why has the writer used BUT here instead of AND? What effect does the contrast create for the reader?
c) "She ran between the stalls because she wanted to find the best fruit." What would the sentence mean if you replaced BECAUSE with BUT? Would the sentence still make sense? Why?
d) The writer used four different conjunctions in just three sentences. Why is using a VARIETY of conjunctions better than repeating the same one? What does it do for the reader?
