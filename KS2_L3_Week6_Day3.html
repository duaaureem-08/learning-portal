⭐ Week 6 English — Key Rules to Remember
📘 Rule 1
Long vowel / 2 consonants → just add: fast → faster.
🔴 Rule 2
Short vowel + 1 consonant → double: hop → hopping.
🟢 Rule 3
Silent e at end → drop the e: bake → baking.
💜 Rule 4
Consonant + y → change y to i: happy → happier.
⚡ Exception
Some words don't follow rules. Learn by heart: caught, bicycle, believe…

⭐ Week 6 Maths — Key Rules
🔄 Use ×
Use your times tables BACKWARDS. If 3 × 4 = 12, then 12 ÷ 3 = 4.
📦 Fact Family
3 × 4 = 12 gives: 4 × 3 = 12, 12 ÷ 3 = 4, 12 ÷ 4 = 3.
✅ Check It
Multiply quotient × divisor. Should equal the dividend.
❓ Missing?
Use inverse: ___ ÷ 5 = 7 → 7 × 5 = ___
