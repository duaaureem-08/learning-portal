⭐ Week 5 English — Key Rules to Remember
🎨 Adjective
Describes a noun. Comes before it: "the bright sun."
📊 Comparative
Compares TWO things. Add -er or "more": taller, more beautiful.
🏆 Superlative
Compares THREE+ things. Add -est or "most": tallest, most beautiful.
📝 Descriptive Writing
Uses adjectives + details to help the reader SEE and FEEL the scene.

⭐ Week 5 Science — Key Ideas
🌱 Germination
Seed + water + warmth + air = growth begins. Root down, shoot up.
🐝 Pollination
Bees and insects carry pollen between flowers. Seeds form.
💨 Seed Dispersal
Wind, water, animals spread seeds far away from parent plant.
🏠 Habitat
The natural home of a living thing. It provides food, water and shelter.
🦎 Adaptation
A feature that helps an animal or plant survive in its habitat.

⭐ Week 5 Social Studies — Key Ideas
🌾 Rural
Countryside. Few people, farms, open space. Example: a village.
🏙️ Urban
City. Many people, tall buildings, busy transport. Example: Karachi, London.
🏘️ Suburban
Between city and countryside. Housing estates near cities.
🤝 Community
People who live, work and share services in the same place.
🏗️ Infrastructure
Roads, schools, hospitals — everything a community needs to work.
